# CDDA 0.G Additive 2026.10.05

Released: 2026-10-05

Changes since [v0.G-additive-2026.08.29.2](https://github.com/asketmc/cdda-asketmc/releases/tag/v0.G-additive-2026.08.29.2).

For the cumulative fork overview, see [PATCHNOTES_ADDITIVE_0G.md](https://github.com/asketmc/cdda-asketmc/blob/main/PATCHNOTES_ADDITIVE_0G.md).

## Player changes

### Features

- Make solar power practical and restore rewarding classic lab and LMOE exploration. ([PR #32](https://github.com/asketmc/cdda-asketmc/pull/32))
  - Solar panels retain irradiance-based output at a 90 W base rating, and the irradiator again contains a rare compact ASRG.
  - Classic labs and ordinary LMOEs are more discoverable on newly generated overmaps, with targeted guaranteed rewards.
  - Save compatibility: Compatible with existing 0.G additive saves.
- Restore rare military turret sites as dangerous, damaged, partially loaded salvage encounters. ([PR #32](https://github.com/asketmc/cdda-asketmc/pull/32))
  - Military roadblocks and outposts can field damaged rifle or heavy-weapon turrets with sharply limited ammunition.
  - Police encounters and turret combat statistics remain unchanged.
  - Save compatibility: Compatible with existing 0.G additive saves.
- Preserve dirty corpse salvage and restore sparse thematic caches as independent CBM scavenging routes. ([PR #33](https://github.com/asketmc/cdda-asketmc/pull/33))
  - Scientists, technicians, military zombies, and bio-operators retain skill-scaled dissection salvage alongside Exodii zomborgs.
  - Hospitals, mines, science sites, Robofac, and selected secure locations again offer low-rate CBM discoveries.
  - Save compatibility: Compatible with existing 0.G additive saves.
- Restore the Railgun and the Integrated Multitool's classic utility without removing later CBMs. ([PR #33](https://github.com/asketmc/cdda-asketmc/pull/33))
  - Railgun returns beside Throwing Assist and consumes 10 kJ only when enhancing a ferric throw.
  - Both Integrated Multitool forms again provide the classic hammering, sawing, wrenching, prying, and drilling qualities.
  - Save compatibility: Compatible with existing 0.G additive saves.
- Restore expert manual CBM installation as an off-by-default world mod with meaningful surgical risk. ([PR #34](https://github.com/asketmc/cdda-asketmc/pull/34))
  - Positive-difficulty CBMs without dedicated procedures require electronics 8, health care 6, mechanics 4, surgical tools, sterile supplies, and anesthetic.
  - Improvised surgery causes pain unless the installer is pain-immune and retains at least a five-percent failure chance with the existing dangerous consequences.
  - Save compatibility: Compatible with existing 0.G additive saves.
- Dissect faster with medical skill, fine-cutting tools, and helpers, and dissect every eligible corpse in corpse-loot zones. ([PR #36](https://github.com/asketmc/cdda-asketmc/pull/36))
  - Dissection time now depends on First Aid, perception, dexterity, fine-cutting quality, and up to three nearby crafting helpers.
  - The zone activity menu gains Dissect corpses with automatic fine-cutting tool fetching, and followers can be ordered to dissect through dialogue without claiming the same corpse.
  - Save compatibility: Compatible with existing 0.G additive saves.
  - Known limit: The activity-specific reality-bubble shrink from the donor is not included because 0.G keeps fixed-size map caches.

### Balance

- Shorten Rubik's restock and trust cadence while keeping tiered sales deterministic and gated. ([PR #34](https://github.com/asketmc/cdda-asketmc/pull/34))
  - Rubik restocks every three days and unlocks CBM tiers at Exodii trust 1, 8, 16, and 30.
  - Exodii installation has a lower service multiplier; other installers and ownership or trading costs remain unchanged.
  - Save compatibility: Compatible with existing 0.G additive saves.

### Bugfixes

- Lab-security corridor updates no longer report mapgen errors when recoloring a tile that contains loot. ([PR #35](https://github.com/asketmc/cdda-asketmc/pull/35))
  - The corridor floor markings now preserve existing loose items, furniture, and traps.
  - Save compatibility: Compatible with existing 0.G additive saves.
